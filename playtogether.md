# PlayTogether — Full Game Suite

Party-game platform: big screen = console, phone = controller. Bots auto-fill empty slots so the arena always feels alive. Below is the full lineup.

---

## Claude's Games

### 1. Color Rush Arena (Flagship)
**Players:** up to 30–40 (real + bots) | **Round:** 60–90 sec
**How it works:** Floor tiles flash random colors; a target color shows at the top. Stand on the matching tile before the timer ends.
**Controls:** Swipe up/down/left/right to move. No jump, no attack.
**Rules:** Wrong color/no color = eliminated (or lose points in points mode). Last player standing / top scorer wins. New round starts instantly.

### 2. Bunny Hop Rally
**Players:** up to 30–40 | **Round:** Multiple short heats
**How it works:** Rabbit characters hop across a shrinking, obstacle-filled platform toward a finish line.
**Controls:** Tap to hop, tilt/swipe left-right to steer mid-air.
**Rules:** Avoid gaps and moving platforms. First few to finish qualify for the next round; arena shrinks each round.

### 3. King of the Zone
**Players:** up to 30–40 | **Round:** 60–90 sec
**How it works:** A glowing zone appears and randomly relocates every 10–15 sec. Standing inside earns points over time.
**Controls:** Swipe to move; bump/push nearby players to knock them out of the zone.
**Rules:** Highest cumulative time-in-zone at round end wins.

## ChatGPT's Games

### 1. Chaos Arena ⭐ (their #1 pick)
**Players:** 2–50 | **Round:** 2–3 min
**How it works:** Survive in a small arena that keeps changing — ice floor, spinning hammers, disappearing tiles, rising lava, low gravity, shrinking circle, etc.
**Controls:** Push, bump, dash, jump — no weapons.
**Rules:** Last player standing wins. Hazards rotate every ~10 sec to keep it chaotic.

### 2. Egg Survivor
**How it works:** Each player is an egg with 3 cracks before elimination. Arena has funny obstacles and random powerups.
**Rules:** Survive the longest without cracking 3 times.

### 3. Penguin Ice Run
**How it works:** Players run forward as breaking ice chases them from behind.
**Rules:** Last penguin still on ice wins.

### 4. Duck Push
**How it works:** Ducks on floating platforms, only push mechanic, water slowly rises.
**Rules:** Get pushed off or submerged = eliminated.

### 5. Tiny Traffic
**How it works:** Drive tiny cars on a road that gets crazier (oil, trains, rain, moving bridges).
**Rules:** Don't crash — survive/finish the course.

### 6. Banana Tag
**How it works:** One player is "the banana"; touching another player passes the banana to them.
**Rules:** Whoever is never tagged by round end wins.

### 7. Tornado City
**How it works:** A moving tornado roams the map while players collect coins.
**Rules:** Avoid the tornado, collect the most coins to unlock cosmetics.

### 8. Sheep Stampede
**How it works:** Hundreds of sheep on screen; players hide among them and scatter randomly.
**Rules:** Find the real players hiding as fake sheep before time runs out.

### 9. Balloon Battle
**How it works:** Players float using balloons; bumping pops opponents' balloons.
**Rules:** Lose all balloons = fall and eliminated.

### 10. Freeze!
**How it works:** One random player is "Frozen" and can freeze others by touch.
**Rules:** Last player still moving/unfrozen wins.

## Gemini's Games

### 1. Lava Sumo Knockout
**Players:** 1–50 | **Round:** 60–90 sec
**How it works:** Arena floats over lava; outer rings crumble over time, forcing players together.
**Controls:** Left thumb = joystick to move, right thumb = SLAP/DASH button to push rivals.
**Rules:** Last player on the platform wins; knocking someone into the lava = +100 bonus. Eliminated players become ghosts who can trigger traps (banana peels, meteors, oil slicks) against survivors.

### 2. Neon Drift Racers
**Players:** 2–8 | **Round:** 3 laps (~2 min)
**How it works:** Top-down neon car racing circuit, camera adjusts to keep all racers visible.
**Controls:** Gyro tilt or touch buttons to steer; DRIFT/NITRO BOOST button.
**Rules:** First to complete 3 laps wins. Drifting builds Nitro; use it to ram opponents off track.

### 3. Bomb Potato Pass
**Players:** 3–16 | **Round:** 3–5 min
**How it works:** One player holds a ticking bomb; passing it requires bumping another player AND completing a 2-second micro-challenge (swipe fast, tap dots, match colors).
**Controls:** 4-way movement pad/joystick; micro-challenge pops up on phone when holding the bomb.
**Rules:** Bomb holder when timer hits 0 is eliminated. Last survivor wins; successful passes earn bonus points.

### 4. Pixel Painter Arena
**Players:** 2–12 (solo or 2v2v2 teams) | **Round:** 2 min
**How it works:** Players move on a grid, automatically painting tiles their color. Power-ups (paint bomb, turbo sprint, ink eraser) spawn randomly.
**Controls:** Joystick to move; item button to trigger power-ups.
**Rules:** Most painted floor tiles when time runs out wins. Stepping on a rival's tile overwrites it with your color.

## General Platform Rules (All Games)
- One arena per session, shared big screen — phone is controller only.
- Join via connect code / QR scan, no extra app installs needed.
- Bots dynamically fill and leave to keep the lobby population steady.
- Rounds are short with instant restart to maximize replay ("one more round" loop).
